# 1. Product brief

- When: 2026-11-12 07:38 UTC
- Led to: The interactive prototype (now `apps/web/`), published as a claude.ai artifact.

````text
Bibbly - translator and work lifestyle app
I have an idea for a AI-powered, lifestyle, voice-to-clarity and translator app (for both mobile and PC/laptop) and plugin that transcribes speech in real time from the input language to the selected output language (e.g., Mandarin/Chinese to English and Vice Versa). 

For the translation capability, I want to ensure it works both ways (or for anyone in a meeting who is speaking). I.e, each user can choose the output language. Also ensure that the app can detect whatever language is being spoken for each speaker. The entire translated output and transcript data can then be saved, stored and exported in file format to any meeting or notes app (Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meets, Notion, Slack, Lark etc) for easy reference and workplace and workflow efficiency.

I also have the further idea to encode this app software into a physical usb drive product that can be used as the recorder itself and can be plugged into PC/laptops for real time usage during online meetings, and can be linked to/used as a connector to the aboventioned workplace toolkits/suites (like Microsoft Suite, Google apps etc).

For the lifestyle component, included the features of tasks, reminders and calendar, taking inspiration from the app: Bobble (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bobble/id6795454754). These features should complement my app's main capability of notetaking and transcription. 

I want the UIUX design to include an iconic character/mascot that represents Bibbly; with the character design that takes inspiration from the features of Bibbles from Barbie: Fairytopia, but is distinct and unique to avoid trademark/copyright issues.
````
